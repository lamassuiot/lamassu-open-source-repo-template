Thank you for your interest in contributing to this project. This agreement applies when a
contribution is made as an employee, contractor, or representative of a company, and that
company may have rights to the contribution. See [legal/README.md](./README.md) for how to
request and submit this agreement, and for the Individual Contributor License Agreement (ICLA)
that applies to contributions made on an individual's own behalf.

An authorized signatory for the company must confirm the company's agreement, and the company
must list or otherwise make identifiable the employees/contractors authorized to contribute
under this agreement.
